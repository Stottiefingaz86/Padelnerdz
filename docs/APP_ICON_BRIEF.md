# App Icon Brief — Padel Nerdz

## Goal
Create a premium, legible app icon for iOS and Android that feels bold, sporty, and social.

## Recommended directions
### Direction A — Crown + PN monogram
- stylized `PN`
- crown above
- high contrast
- simpler and more scalable for tiny sizes

### Direction B — Crown over simplified wordmark
- crown above a much simplified `padel nerdz` lockup
- use only if legibility remains strong at 60x60 and smaller

## Color suggestions
- dark background + white mark
- optional energetic accent for crown or small detail

## Must-have qualities
- instantly recognizable
- works on light and dark phone wallpapers
- crisp at small sizes
- consistent with energetic logo personality

## Do not
- cram the full expressive logo if it becomes unreadable
- use too many colors
- make it too detailed
